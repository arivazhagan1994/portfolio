Profile Overview

A unique journey from industrial engineering to artificial intelligence

Mechanical Engineer → Data Science & AI

I am a Mechanical Engineering diploma holder with over 7 years of experience as a Boiler Operator in the industrial sector. I have hands-on expertise in managing firetube boilers, steam generation systems, pressure control, and optimizing fuel efficiency in large-scale operations.

I have actively worked on energy management, sustainability initiatives, and emission tracking, including CO₂ emission calculations, specific energy consumption (SEC/SGE), and performance monitoring aligned with industrial standards.

I am experienced in calculating baseline emissions, achieved reductions, and year-on-year performance improvements, supporting ESG and sustainability reporting through data-driven analysis.

Currently, I am transitioning into Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, where I combine my industrial knowledge with Python, Machine Learning, and data visualization to build real-world applications such as energy dashboards, emission analysis systems, and predictive models.

My goal is to develop intelligent, data-driven solutions that improve operational efficiency, reduce emissions, and support sustainable industrial transformation.

Bridging industrial engineering with AI to create smarter, more sustainable systems.
